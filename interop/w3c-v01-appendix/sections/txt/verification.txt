Each v0.1 report in the table can be checked with one command, from an empty directory,

uvx agent-evidence-vectors==0.32.1 --read-w3c-report <file>

which prints conforms, or one line per shape error or rejection row, and exits 0 when the report conforms, 1 when it does not, and 2 when the reader reached no verdict. Rows that are not reports (readers' verdicts, comparisons, census counts) are checked by their rerun command instead. The evidence classes are their authors' words: "reproduction in O8's sense" (Oct/0163), "a reader written from the 0087 text alone" and "proposed" (Oct/0162), "the closest this paper comes to an independent record" (Oct/0150). The class of every cell this appendix runs is "automated rerun from the pins, operated by the appendix's author". Following Oct/0163, units differ by row and no column is summed.

Negative controls for the readers.. A reader that accepted everything could match the conforming base reports, so the matrix also records single-field negative controls. The generated controls table gives the denominator, outcomes and target-citation counts for each reader. Positive controls and targets marked not applicable are separate populations; neither belongs in that negative-control denominator. The positive controls use a non-ASCII check identity and a root recomputed over the RFC 8785 form. Their ASCII-escaped twins target W3C-R-020, the cause Kenne Ives gave for R4 (Oct/0150). A declared moved that disagrees with the recomputed value targets W3C-R-021; a recomputed moved outside compared targets W3C-R-016.

The readers written from the text were built to answer cannot-tell where the text leaves the reading open (Oct/0039), and the controls show where that stops: over all negative controls each also accepts copies aimed at requirements outside numbered rows 1 to 14, and a reader that computes no tree root cannot reject a changed root (Nov/0028). The generated target-population table retains the separate outcome classes, including positive controls. The older Python readers accept the ASCII-escaped W3C-R-020 twins and reject their RFC 8785 positive controls. For the text-derived readers, the shared numbered-row control not rejected is R1 with a qualifier on a non-verdict check, targeting row 6. Detailed cells are retained in the generated matrix/matrix.md. The command list gives ways to check the reports; it is not an additional set of recorded reader cells. Target cited counts whether the named target was cited, not merely whether a mutant was rejected. These are findings for their authors, not faults.
