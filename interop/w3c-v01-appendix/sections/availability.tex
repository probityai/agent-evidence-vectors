\paragraph{Package releases.} The reference emitter and readers are the \texttt{agent-evidence-vectors} package on PyPI, installed at an exact version: 0.12.0 (R1), 0.13.0, 0.17.4 and 0.18.1 (the readers R14 names), 0.31.0 (the newest release on 1 November 2026, in the reader matrix), and \aevreadrelease, which adds the one-line reader command \texttt{--read-w3c-report} used in the verification section. R3 also has its own archive, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21874026 (\aevmsg{Oct}{0162}).

\paragraph{List attachments.} R2, R3, R3-v01, R8, R8-compare and R11 run on files attached to list messages (\aevmsg{Oct}{0063}, \aevmsg{Oct}{0060}, \aevmsg{Nov}{0000}, \aevmsg{Oct}{0039}, \aevmsg{Oct}{0188}, \aevmsg{Oct}{0139}). The harness fetches each from the archive and checks it against the sha256 posted in its message.

\paragraph{The bundle.} A Zenodo DOI, 10.5281/zenodo.23873877, is reserved for one deposit of the appendix, its pinned inputs and its run records. It is not published. Its creators follow the paper's final author list, and it is published only on the authors' approval, as with the paper itself (\aevmsg{Nov}{0001}).
