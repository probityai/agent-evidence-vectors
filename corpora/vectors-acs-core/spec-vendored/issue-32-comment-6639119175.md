1. Yes, the #308 text is the split. A step ends one of three ways:
   - A verified decision is honored.
   - No usable answer follows on_decision_failure: the Guardian is silent past the timeout, the connection or TLS handshake fails, HTTP 408, 429 or 5xx arrives with no JSON-RPC body, or the Guardian answers with a signed -32603.
   - A refusal or an integrity failure blocks the step whatever the posture: any unsigned error, any signed error other than -32603, a bad signature or unknown key_id, a JSON-RPC id or request_id that doesn't match, or a result that fails response-envelope.json, an allow included. It is recorded with its failure class. A retry is a new request, judged on its own.
2. The default becomes deny, as the leads decided on 3 November. The startup posture in §4.1 follows: a session whose handshake gets no answer refuses its steps unless the deployment sets proceed. A Guardian that declares proceed still gets proceed.
3. Yes. Any HTTP 4xx except 408 and 429 is a refusal, 401 and 403 included. @Santoshkumarpuppala, the post() fix can go ahead on that rule. A TLS alert stays a transport failure, since it can't be told apart from an outage.
