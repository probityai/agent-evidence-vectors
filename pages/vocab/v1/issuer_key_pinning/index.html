issuer_key_pinning

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/issuer_key_pinning

Section: principal_identity. Criticality: critical.

Whether the client that obtained the token pinned the issuer's signing key by its RFC 7638 thumbprint before its first call. A pin is all or nothing: a configuration that pins one issuer and leaves another unpinned, or names two thumbprints for one issuer, is malformed and is never read as pinned. A pin is never downgraded: on a thumbprint mismatch or a failed key fetch the client refuses, and does not fall back to the unpinned path. Closed and fail-closed.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/issuer_key_pinning#pinned_before_first_call: The client held the RFC 7638 SHA-256 thumbprint of the issuer key in its configuration before its first call to the issuer and refused any key whose thumbprint differed.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/issuer_key_pinning#unpinned: The client trusted whatever key the issuer's JWKS served at the time, with no thumbprint configured.
