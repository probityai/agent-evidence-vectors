lifecycle_binding

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/lifecycle_binding

Section: evaluation_claim. Criticality: critical.

Whether the authenticated execution report names the action the authenticated control authorized, for one decision and one instruction. Records are grouped by (decision, instruction). Every authorizing record in the group, from the issuing and the receiving side, is compared with every execution record in the group; a match on one pair proves nothing about the others. Each digest is computed by the convention declared for its pointer (raw or a named canonicalization), the signature suite is the one the verifier-accepted key binding names (the wire algorithm member is informative only), the key binding is resolved from the record's producer, and a revocation snapshot is required. Hash and signature pre-images are domain-separated: each is a tag naming the format version, the artifact type and whether it is a hash or a signature input, a line feed, then the canonical bytes, and the tags come from a closed registry, so an unregistered tag fails closed and one signature can never be replayed as another artifact type or as a hash. The registry is exactly six ASCII tags of the form agent-evidence/lifecycle-binding/v1/TYPE/PURPOSE, TYPE one of decision, control or execution and PURPOSE one of hash or signature; the pre-image is the tag bytes, one byte 0x0A, then the canonical bytes, with nothing between them and nothing after. A v2 format is a new set of tags, never a respelling of these. Absent evidence is never satisfied. The claim is about records: it never says that the executed bytes themselves were observed.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/lifecycle_binding#satisfied: Every artifact in the group is admitted, authenticated under an active key binding and unique by identity, and every authorized action digest equals every executed action digest.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/lifecycle_binding#failure: The authorized and executed digests differ among admitted, authenticated, unique, actively bound artifacts, an authorizing record disagrees with another authorizing record in the group, or an artifact in the group, or the decision it cites, does not authenticate (its signature fails, or its key is not the one its producer's binding names).
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/lifecycle_binding#missing: The group lacks an authorizing record, an execution record, or the decision record it cites.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/lifecycle_binding#not_evaluated: An artifact needed for the comparison, or the decision it cites, is signed under a key binding that is not active in the supplied revocation snapshot, or the execution cites an instruction no authorization in the supplied set covers.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/lifecycle_binding#indeterminate: Two artifacts in the group share one identity; no winner is chosen and nothing is compared.
