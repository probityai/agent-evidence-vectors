topology_decision_basis

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/topology_decision_basis

Section: multi_agent_run. Criticality: critical.

What label or input steered one topology decision. A decision whose basis begins detector_ carries a decimal risk estimate for every agent and the list of message fields the detector read. Closed and fail-closed: an absent or unregistered basis makes the decision malformed.
