decision_verdict

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/decision_verdict

Section: decision_receipt. Criticality: critical.

The outcome a decision point reached for one proposed action, in a fixed strictness order from least to most strict: allow, constrain, require_approval, block, halt. When more than one policy answers the same action, the strictest verdict wins, the same way in every verifier, and a verifier reports every policy that answered at the winning verdict. A check that failed without producing a verdict of its own, such as a content guardrail, is read as block. An unregistered verdict, a differently spelled one and an empty set of answers are never read as allow. Closed and fail-closed.
