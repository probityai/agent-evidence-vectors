upstream_receipt

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/upstream_receipt

Section: decision_receipt. Criticality: critical.

One fact about a call, stated by the agent platform in its receipt and re-checkable at the independent party that served the call. The receipt names the provider, the provider's own identifier for the call (a generation id) and an https re-check address. A verifier checks the one fact against the provider's record without trusting the agent platform or the producer of the receipt. The result is reported as one of four values, and only agrees is a pass: unreachable and not_checked are never counted as pass. Every failure to obtain a record, whether the call is unknown, the record expired, the address answered with something other than a record or the transport failed, is reported as unreachable with one error text, so the answer reveals nothing about which.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/upstream_receipt#disagrees: The verifier asked the upstream provider and its record differs from the fact the receipt states.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/upstream_receipt#unreachable: The verifier asked and the provider did not answer with a record, for any reason, including a record that has expired.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/upstream_receipt#not_checked: The verifier did not ask.
