key_history

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_history

Section: record_binding. Criticality: critical.

The standing of a signing key as of the time a record was issued, read from a key history the verifier holds (authorization start, rotation end, revocation with its effective time and reason). It separates a key rotated out after issuance, which leaves the record valid, from a key that was never authorized at issuance, which makes it a forgery. Without that separation a pre-rotation record and a forgery look the same, and a verifier either refuses every old record or accepts every forged one.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_history#key_current: The signing key was authorized when the record was issued, an authorization window that holds the issuance time is still open, and the history records no revocation of the key.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_history#key_rotated_out: The signing key was authorized when the record was issued, and every window that held the issuance time has since ended, by an ordinary rotation or by a revocation whose effective time is after issuance. The record stays valid.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_history#key_not_authorized_at_issue: The signing key is known to the history, but its authorization began after the record's issuance time or ended before it by rotation. The record is refused.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_history#key_revoked: The signing key was revoked at or before the record's issuance time, or revoked afterwards for compromise with an effective time at or before issuance. The record is refused.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_history#key_unknown: The verifier holds no history that names the signing key. Nothing is decided: the result is indeterminate, never refused as forged and never accepted.
