restoration_decision

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/restoration_decision

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

The decision that ends a recorded containment, bound to it by digest, with an approver identity different from the actor who activated the containment. A missing element makes the verdict not_established. A decision not to resume is remain_restricted, and it is recorded the same way as a resumption.
