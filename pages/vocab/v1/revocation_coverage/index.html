revocation_coverage

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/revocation_coverage

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

What share of the issuance population a revocation reached. The population is the set of credentials, tokens, sessions and delegations issued to the subject, and the record MUST cite a coverage_denominator that partitions it against a manifest the revoker does not control. This is a fact about the population and not about enforcement: revocation_state says, for one relying party, whether a revoked credential was refused, and this says how much of the population the revocation covered. Closed and fail-closed: an absent or unregistered value makes the statement malformed.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/revocation_coverage#complete_verified: The revocation reached every credential in the issuance population, and the record carries a revocation for each one the manifest names.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/revocation_coverage#issued_after_only: The revocation reached only credentials issued at or after a stated time; credentials issued earlier were not revoked.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/revocation_coverage#partial: The revocation reached a named subset of the issuance population, and the record names the subset.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/revocation_coverage#unknown: The record does not establish which part of the issuance population the revocation reached.
