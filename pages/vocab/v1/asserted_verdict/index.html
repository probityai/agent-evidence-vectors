asserted_verdict

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/asserted_verdict

Section: evaluation_claim. Criticality: critical.

The outcome of checking a verdict a record asserts about its own inputs. A signature over an asserted verdict proves who asserted it, never that it is right: the key that is entitled to sign can sign a verdict its own inputs refute. So an asserted verdict is read only through re-derivation, and a verifier that trusts the asserted field accepts every such record.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/asserted_verdict#rederived_agrees: The verifier re-derived the verdict from the recorded inputs under the named evaluator and got the verdict the record asserts.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/asserted_verdict#asserted_verdict_contradicted: The re-derived verdict differs from the asserted one. The record is refused, whoever signed it.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/asserted_verdict#evaluator_unknown: The record names no evaluator, or one the verifier does not hold. The asserted verdict is reported and nothing is decided.
