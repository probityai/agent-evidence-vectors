restoration_gate

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/restoration_gate

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

Whether an observed restart passed the gate that restoration_decision sets. restoration_decision records the decision; this records whether the restart that actually happened was preceded by one that met its requirements. The restart time comes from the substrate, not from the party that restarted.
