incident_identifier

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/incident_identifier

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

An identifier for one incident, carried in the records that each party signs, so that two organisations' records of the same incident can be joined. The first party to open the incident mints it as an issuer and an id. A party that learns of the incident from another adopts the identifier by listing it in a related array and keeps its own id; nobody rewrites a signed record to change an identifier. Two records concern one incident when they carry the same issuer and id, or when one lists the other's in related, and the join is the transitive closure of that relation.
