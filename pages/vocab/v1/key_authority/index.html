key_authority

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_authority

Section: record_binding. Criticality: critical.

What proves that the key a signature verifies under is entitled to speak for the record. A signature proves only that some key signed some bytes. A key carried inside the record, or fetched over a channel the attacker controls, proves nothing about who signed: a forger signs with their own key and ships it alongside. A verdict that the record agrees with its signer needs one of the first three values; none tops out at cannot tell, however well the signature verifies.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_authority#pin: The verifier holds the signer's public key, or its thumbprint, from a source outside the record and the record's own transport.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_authority#dnssec_chain: The key is published in DNS and the verifier validated the DNSSEC chain from a trust anchor it holds to the record that carries the key.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_authority#kt_inclusion: The key is in a key-transparency log and the verifier checked an inclusion proof against a log head signed by a key it holds.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/key_authority#none: The key came only from the record itself or from an unauthenticated fetch.
