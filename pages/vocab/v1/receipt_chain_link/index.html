receipt_chain_link

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/receipt_chain_link

Section: decision_receipt. Criticality: critical.

Whether a record names, by digest, the record its signer emitted before it, so that deleting a record breaks every record after it. Individually valid records do not make a set of records whole: a decision point can decline to emit a record for a decision it would rather forget. A chain proves its middle and its start; it proves nothing about newer records cut from the end, so a verifier states the end it could not establish unless the consumer holds the last digest and the count outside the signer's reach. A chain is read in order, a repeated record is refused, and two records that claim the same predecessor are a fork.
