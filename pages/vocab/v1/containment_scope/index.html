containment_scope

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/containment_scope

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

A digest-committed partition of the channels a contained subject could use (network routes, credentials, tools, shared stores, peer agents) into cut, retained-with-reason and unassessed, whose union MUST equal a channel manifest the subject does not control, with the interval [effectiveAt, observedUntil] over which each cut channel was observed closed. A containment with a non-empty unassessed set is reported as partial, never as complete.
