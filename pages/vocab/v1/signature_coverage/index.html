signature_coverage

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/signature_coverage

Section: record_binding. Criticality: critical.

How much of a record a verifying signature actually covers. A valid signature that covers part of a record is a true statement about that part and no statement about the rest, and reporting it as plain verified is a false statement about what was proven: whoever chooses which genuine signature to replay chooses one that leaves the members they want to change uncovered. A key that could not be fetched leaves the question unanswered, which is cannot tell and never none.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/signature_coverage#full: The signature verifies and covers every member of the record a consumer acts on.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/signature_coverage#endpoint_only: The signature verifies and covers only the endpoint members (name, target, port, protocol); the other members a consumer acts on are not attested.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/signature_coverage#none: No signature covers the record, or the signature that is present does not verify.
