revocation_state

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/revocation_state

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

Where a revocation of a credential or grant stands at one relying party at one time. Issuing a revocation and enforcing it are different facts, and a record states which one it holds.
