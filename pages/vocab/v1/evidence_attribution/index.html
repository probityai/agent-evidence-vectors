evidence_attribution

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/evidence_attribution

Section: evaluation_claim. Criticality: critical.

Which parties a record binds. Two signatures bind two parties to one record. They do not prove the record true, and they do not prove the two parties independent: one operator can hold both keys, and two parties can agree on a false statement. A co-signature that is present and does not verify is not a weaker co-signature; it makes the record unattributed evidence, because the record claims a binding it does not have. A signature under a key that may not speak for its party binds no party either, so the value is decided with key authority: a forger who signs as the agent under a key of his own makes the record unattributed evidence, not co-signed.
