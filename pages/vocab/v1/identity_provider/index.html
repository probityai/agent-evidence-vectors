identity_provider

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/identity_provider

Section: principal_identity. Criticality: critical.

The identity provider that operates the issuer of the token a decision point validated, derived from principal.issuer. principal.source keeps naming the kind of token; this term names who issued it, so one closed set covers the four hosted and self-hosted providers agents meet most and the case where nobody but the deployer vouches. Closed and fail-closed.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/identity_provider#entra: The issuer is Microsoft Entra ID or Entra External ID: an https issuer on login.microsoftonline.com, login.microsoftonline.us, login.chinacloudapi.cn, sts.windows.net or a host ending in ciamlogin.com, or a custom domain the record declares as entra.
