revocation_list_in_force

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/revocation_list_in_force

Section: decision_receipt. Criticality: critical.

Which revocation data the decision point held when it decided. Holding no list and holding a list that revoked nothing are different claims: the first says revocation was never consulted, the second says it was consulted and did not match. A list that revoked nothing still has a digest, the digest of the empty list's canonical bytes, and so is never no_list_loaded. Without this term a stale or absent list is indistinguishable from a current one.
