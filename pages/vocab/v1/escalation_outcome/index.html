escalation_outcome

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/escalation_outcome

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

Whether a detection alert reached someone able to act in time. The record carries alertAt (when the monitor raised the alert), pagedAt (when a named responder was notified), actionAt (the effective time of the first response action, or of a continue decision) and the policy bound in seconds, so the delay from alert to action is computable from the record and is not a prose claim.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/escalation_outcome#paged_late: A named responder was notified, after the stated bound.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/escalation_outcome#not_paged: The alert reached no responder with authority to act.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/escalation_outcome#not_established: The alert time, the notification time or the bound is missing, or comes only from the responder's own system.
