signer_distinctness

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/signer_distinctness

Section: evaluation_claim. Criticality: critical.

Whether the signers a record counts toward a required number are distinct, both as keys and as authorities. A threshold of signatures is worth what the number of independent authorities behind it is worth: two identifiers for one key, the issuer counted as its own cosigner, or a threshold met by keys of one authority each meet the count and none of them meets the purpose; a count that is simply short is a fifth failure. Applies to agent capability credentials, threshold-signed receipts and any record that requires more than one signer.
