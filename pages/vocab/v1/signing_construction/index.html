signing_construction

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/signing_construction

Section: record_binding. Criticality: critical.

Which bytes a signature over an agent catalog object is computed on. One object family is signed three different ways in normative text and in the field, and a record valid under one construction is invalid or not applicable under the others. The construction is therefore a named, closed parameter of every signature verdict over such an object: a verdict that names no construction cannot be reproduced, and a verifier that tries each construction until one verifies accepts whatever an attacker can make verify under any of them.
