verification_decision

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/verification_decision

Section: evaluation_claim. Criticality: critical.

The verifier's decision on one record, with a third outcome beside accept and reject. A two-valued decision forces an undecidable record into one of the two: read as accepted it lets an unknown key through, read as rejected it reports a forgery that is not there. indeterminate is a decision in its own right, carried in the verifier's exit status (0 accepted, 1 rejected, 2 indeterminate) and in its report, and never counted as a pass.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/verification_decision#accepted: Every check the profile requires ran and passed.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/verification_decision#rejected: At least one check ran and failed.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/verification_decision#indeterminate: No check failed, and at least one required check could not be decided from what the verifier holds (an unknown evaluator, an unknown key, an unreachable source).
