Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

Whether a pause held. An effect by the paused subject is an effect whose operation the subject initiated at or after effectiveAt; state the subject left behind is anything it created before effectiveAt. The two are different values because only the first shows the pause failed to stop the subject; the second shows what remains to be remediated. When several apply the record carries the first in this order: effect_after_pause, in_flight_unknown, residual_state_remains, held.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/pause_hold#held: The substrate observed no effect by the paused subject after effectiveAt, and no state it created before effectiveAt remains active.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/pause_hold#in_flight_unknown: An operation the subject started before effectiveAt has no confirmed outcome.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/pause_hold#effect_after_pause: An effect caused by an operation the paused subject initiated carries a time after effectiveAt.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/pause_hold#residual_state_remains: No effect by the paused subject after effectiveAt, but state it created before effectiveAt (an account, credential, pod or job) is still active.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/pause_hold#not_established: There was no substrate observation.
