block_time_allowance

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/block_time_allowance

Section: evaluation_claim. Criticality: critical.

The seconds added to a time bound to cover the uncertainty of the clock behind an anchor, stated per bound. A block-chain header time trails the commitment it carries by confirmation latency and may be set loosely by the miner, so a bound compared against it needs an allowance; a bound compared against a clock that states its own accuracy does not. The allowance belongs to the bound it widens and is never shared: this term widens the late anchor bound, anchor_early_allowance is the early anchor bound's own, and neither widens the future bound on issuance.
