done_receipt

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/done_receipt

Section: done_verification. Criticality: critical.

The record of one done decision: the contract's digest, the final tree's digest, the observation of the agent's run, the verifier's grade, the agent's tool claims, and the verdict the three checks reach. It is a replay bundle, so a party who distrusts the producer can rerun the checks on the same bytes.
