response_decision

Term URI: https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/response_decision

Section: incident_response. Criticality: critical.

The decision taken at one incident decision point, recorded whether or not anything was done. A decision not to act is a first-class value, so a non-action is a signed, checkable record and not an absence: a reader of a log that shows no action cannot tell a considered continue from an alert nobody saw. Closed and fail-closed: an absent or unregistered value makes the statement malformed.

Registered values

  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/response_decision#act: The decision was to take at least one response action; the record carries the action and its response_function values.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/response_decision#continue: The decision was to take no action and let the activity run on; the record carries the authority that decided and the reason.
  • https://w3id.org/agent-evidence/vocab/v1/response_decision#escalate: The decision was to hand the matter to a party with wider authority; the record names that party.
