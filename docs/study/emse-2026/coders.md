# Code 27 changelog entries by cause

Part of the study "Agreement Is Not Independence", for the Empirical Software Engineering special issue on agentic software engineering.

**Reply by:** 2026-10-08 for codings that go into the paper

Would you help code 27 changelog entries for what caused each change?

For a study of the conformance corpus behind in-toto/attestation#570, each of the corpus's 27 revisions after the first needs a label for what surfaced it: an outside implementer, a comparison between the author's own verifiers, a mutation measurement, an adversarial finding, the suite's own checks, or a specification change. Both existing codings came from one AI model, and they disagree on which cause leads, so the answer has to come from coders who did not write the corpus.

The materials are a short codebook, the 27 entries, and links to the commits behind them. You would label each entry twice: once from its own text, and once after reading its commits; the two are reported separately. More than one label per entry is allowed where the text supports it.

Nothing needs installing, and the answer sheet is a plain table, one row per revision. No knowledge of the specification is needed, and you would not see the existing codings, the other coders' labels or the draft paper until yours is in. Codings back by 2026-10-08 go into the paper. You can stop whenever you like.

You get the results and acknowledgement in the paper by name or anonymously, as you prefer. The consent text is below; reply where you were invited if you are in, and I will send the materials.

## How to take part

Reply where you were invited to say you are in. The codebook, the 27 entries, the commit links and the answer sheet follow by reply.
